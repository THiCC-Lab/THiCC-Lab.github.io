Meera Ray is a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at Penn State and a graduate research assistant in THiCC Lab. She graduated with a B.S. in Statistics and Machine Learning from Carnegie Mellon in 2023. Her research aims to articulate how artificial intelligence interacts with social hierarchies. Meera’s current research project, developing a cognitive model of human decision making in disaster situations, is with the <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/ai-sdm/">AI Institute for Societal Decision Making (AI-SDM)</a>.
