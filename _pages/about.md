<div class="h1" style="font-size: 4rem; text-align: center;">Computing & cognition in the context of <span style="font-style: italic;">the human</span></div><div style="color: #999; margin: 1rem;">*Note that our site is currently under construction and being migrated from <a href="https://sites.psu.edu/thicc/">our previous site</a>, but please feel free to take a look around here or there!*<br /></div>
<div style="font-size: 1.25rem;"> Welcome to The Human in Computing and Cognition THiCC Lab! This lab exists to study, understand, and sometimes reimagine ways that Computing and Cognitive systems exist in the context of humans, especially <a href="https://www.jstor.org/stable/41949874?seq=1#metadata_info_tab_contents">the Human</a>. We do so primarily through developing <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/8669875">computational cognitive models</a>, studying existing computing systems and engineering new ones, and contextualizing sociocultural theory. When it comes to contextualizing our work with sociocultural theory, we tend to focus on perspectives that center <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9606203">blackness</a> (<a href="https://www.dukeupress.edu/antiblackness">and antiblackness</a>).</div>
