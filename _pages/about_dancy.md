<span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.1em;">Dr. Chris Dancy</span> is an Associate Professor of Industrial Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, and African American Studies, and is Director of THiCC lab, co-Director of <a href="https://digblk.psu.edu/">the Center for Black Digital Research/#DgBlk</a>, and the co-lead of the <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/ai-sdm/research/index.html">Computational Representation of Human Decision Processes research thrust</a> in <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/ai-sdm/index.html">the NSF AI Institute for Societal Decision Making</a>. His interest and expertise lies at the intersections of human behavior, computational systems, and sociocultural structures. He uses theoretical perspectives from computational cognitive science and Black studies to ground his research at these intersections. To study the processes that define these interactions, Dr. Dancy uses computational cognitive modeling, human behavioral studies, and design & implementation considerations of computational (particularly “AI”) systems.
