Emmanuel Adjei Domfeh is a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Penn State University and a Graduate Research Assistant at the THICC Lab. He holds a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Philosophy in Computer Science from the University of Energy and Natural Resources, Ghana, where he previously served as a Teaching and Research Assistant and later as an Assistant Lecturer. Emmanuel’s research is rooted in Human-Centered AI, with a focus on designing Reliable, Safe, and Trustworthy AI systems. His current work centers on projects working with the <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/ai-sdm/">AI Institute of Social Decision Making (AI-SDM)</a>, which explores Human-AI use patterns in disaster recovery. The objectives of his research include: 1.Understanding the diverse Human-AI use patterns in disaster recovery scenarios. 2.Developing computational decision models to optimize interactions between humans and AI during critical decision-making processes. 3.Creating virtual environments and simulations to illustrate and analyze these use patterns in disaster scenarios.
