✊🏽📖 Dr. Dancy, Deja Workman, Dr. Darakhshan Mir, and Dr. Vanessa Massaro organized a symposium focusing on incarceration and carceral algorithms in collaboration with [Restorative Justice Initiative](https://ed.psu.edu/academics/restorative-justice-initiative) and the [PA Prison Data Collective](https://carceral.netlify.app/). We brought together people from various backgrounds, including those who are justice-impacted, academics, and more. Our theme – past, present, and future – brought out both the unique differences of our work and the similarities that unite us in the mission of bringing attention to the impacts of carceral algorithms.
