✊🏽📖 Dr. Dancy, along with Deja workman, gave a talk at Swathmore College as a part of the Just Tech: Speaker Series. They had the opportunity to present, and engage in critical discussions, on "AI, Archives, and Blackness". Deja talked about some work being done within the [Liberatory Tech Project](https://liberatorytech.org/). Special congrats to PhD Candidate Deja Workman for the first of what I'm sure will be many of such opportunities!
